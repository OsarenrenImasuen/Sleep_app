About Dreambetter

Dreambetter is a sleep-gamification app inspired by the idea that consistent, quality sleep should be celebrated and rewarded. Log your sleep each morning, earn XP based on how long you slept, and level up through our sleep tier system. Use tokens you earn to buy EXP boosters and accelerate your progress.

How XP works:
Each hour of sleep earns 10 XP. Sleeping 7–9 hours earns a bonus. Active EXP boosters multiply your total gain. Every night you also earn 1 token per hour slept.
